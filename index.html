    What the list shows

    Seven of the ten tallest active players are goalkeepers and two are center-backs. England's Kyle Hudlin is the only striker. Clubs tend to spot unusually tall teenagers early and steer them toward the positions where reach matters most.

    Playing soccer doesn't make anyone taller. Adult height comes mostly from genetics, and the position simply follows.
